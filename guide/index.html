Tool rules

ToolHow longRules
CupsStatic 3–5 min first time, never over 10. Gliding 1–2 min an area.Half squeeze to start. Oil or lotion for gliding. Remove by pressing the skin at the edge. Marks fade over several days; don't re-cup a marked spot.
Scraper30–60 sec a spot, max 2–3 min a muscle group, 2–3 times a week.Lotion first. Hold it at 30–45°. Short strokes, one direction. Pink skin is normal. Bruising means too hard.
Peanut ball30–60 sec a spot, held or small rolls.Spine goes in the gap, never on the ball. Zing or tingling means you're on a nerve; move off.
Strap20–30 sec, 2–3 times a side.Best when warm, after activity. No bouncing. Stretch to tension, never sharp pain.

Never use cups or the scraper on: bones, spine, joints, front or sides of the neck, broken skin, rashes, bruises, varicose veins, or a fresh or swollen injury. Ask a doctor first if you take blood thinners, have a bleeding or skin condition, or are pregnant.

Stop and get it checked

Sharp or stabbing pain, swelling, bruising you didn't cause, numbness or tingling, pain inside a joint, a pop, pain that changes how you throw or run, or soreness getting worse after 2–3 days. See your athletic trainer or a doctor.
