The guides

How to use every tool

The basics from the full online guide that comes with every kit, with a video for each one. One rule sits on top of all of them: discomfort is fine, sharp pain means stop.

Silicone cups

Static 3–5 min
[Video: silicone cups, static and gliding]
  1. Rub a little lotion or oil on the area.
  2. Squeeze, place on the muscle, let go. Start with a half squeeze.
  3. Static: 3–5 minutes the first time, never more than 10.
  4. Gliding: with oil, slide slowly 1–2 minutes per area.
  5. Press the skin at the edge to remove. Never pull straight off.

Round marks are normal and fade over several days. Don’t cup that spot again until they’re gone.

Steel scraper

30–60 sec per spot
[Video: scraping forearms and calves]
  1. Lotion on first. The edge should glide, not drag.
  2. Hold the edge at about a 30–45° angle to the skin.
  3. Short strokes in one direction, light to moderate pressure.
  4. No more than 2–3 minutes per muscle group, 2–3 times a week.

Pink skin is normal. Bruising means you pressed too hard.

Peanut ball

30–60 sec per spot
[Video: peanut ball, upper back and glutes]
  1. Pin the ball between the muscle and a wall or the floor.
  2. Find a tight spot. Hold while breathing slowly, or roll in small movements.
  3. Upper back: lie on it with your spine in the gap between the halves.

Don’t roll on the spine, bones, the front of the neck or behind the knee. A zing or tingling means you’re on a nerve. Move off it.

Stretch strap

20–30 sec holds
[Video: strap stretches]
  1. Stretch after activity, while you’re warm.
  2. Use the loops to pull gently. Move one loop further as you loosen up.
  3. Hold 20–30 seconds, 2–3 times per side. No bouncing.

Stretch to tension, never into sharp pain.

Never use the cups or scraper on

Bones, the spine or joints · the front or sides of the neck · broken skin, rashes or bruises · varicose veins · a fresh or swollen injury. Ask a doctor first if you take blood thinners, have a bleeding or skin condition, or are pregnant.

Basic moves by body area

One move for every spot

Start here when one area is talking to you. Light pressure the first time, and stop if anything feels sharp.

UB

Upper back

Peanut ball

On your back, knees bent, ball across your upper back with your spine in the gap. Hands behind your head, lift your hips a little and roll 2–3 inches at a time.

60–90 sec
LT

Lats

Peanut ball

Lie on your side, bottom arm straight overhead. Ball just below the armpit, toward your back. Roll down toward the bottom of your ribs and back. Stay off the armpit itself.

60 sec a side
FA

Forearms

Scraper

Forearm palm-up on your thigh, lotion on. Scraper at 30–45°, short strokes from wrist toward elbow, always the same direction. Skip the bony spots.

30–60 sec an arm
GL

Glutes

Peanut ball

Sit on one end of the ball under one glute, cross that ankle over the other knee and lean toward it. Hold tender spots or roll in small circles.

60 sec a side
HF

Hip flexors

Strap

Kneel on one knee (pad it). Squeeze that glute and shift your hips forward with your chest tall. Loop the strap on the back foot to pull it in for more.

20–30 sec, 2x a side
QD

Quads

Gliding cups

Oil on, half-squeeze a large cup above the knee and glide up toward the hip. Lift, return, three lines across the thigh. Stay off the kneecap.

1–2 min a leg
HM

Hamstrings

Strap

On your back, strap around the ball of one foot. Pull the straight leg up until you feel tension. Hold, then let it fall slightly in and out.

20–30 sec, 2x a side
CF

Calves

Scraper

Foot up on a bench, lotion on. Short strokes from above the ankle to below the knee. Stay off the shin bone and the back of the knee.

30–60 sec a leg

FAQ

Questions, answered

The kit

How is this different from buying the stuff on Amazon?

You could piece it together from five listings. We picked tools that work together, put them in one bag, and added the guide and videos so you actually know what to do with them tonight.

I’ve never done cupping or scraping. Is it hard?

No. Start light: a half-squeeze on the cups for 3–5 minutes, and light pressure with the scraper. The QR code on card 8 opens a video of each step.

Will cupping leave marks?

Usually, yes. Round marks are normal and fade over several days. Don’t cup the same spot again until they’re gone.

Do I need anything else?

Just lotion or oil for the scraper and gliding cups. Whatever you have at home works.

How do I clean everything?

Warm soapy water for the cups, ball and strap. Soap and water or rubbing alcohol for the steel scraper. Air dry.

Safety

Is this medical treatment?

No. These are recovery tools for general wellness. They aren’t meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. If you’re hurt, see an athletic trainer or doctor.

Who shouldn’t use cupping or scraping?

Skip them if you take blood thinners, have a bleeding or skin condition, or are pregnant, unless your doctor says it’s fine. Never use them on broken skin, bruises, varicose veins or a fresh injury.

What age is the kit for?

Athletes 13 and up. Players under 18 should use it with a parent’s okay. It isn’t designed for kids 12 and under.

I’m buying this for my son or daughter. How do they use it safely?

Every kit comes with eight pocket guide cards, plus a step-by-step online guide covering how long to use each tool, where not to use it, and when to stop and see an athletic trainer, plus a video for every step.
